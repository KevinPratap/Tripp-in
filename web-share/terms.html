Using Trippin

Trippin is provided as is, free of charge. You are responsible for what you add to a trip and for checking opening hours, prices, safety and travel rules before you go.

Plans are suggestions

Routes, timings, weather and risk estimates are generated from public data and can be wrong or out of date. Do not rely on them for anything safety critical.

Shared content

Anything you add to a trip is visible to the people on it. If a trip owner turns on the live web page, a public copy is visible to anyone with the link. Do not post anything you do not have the right to share, or anything unlawful, abusive or misleading.

Accounts

Keep your sign in details private. You can delete your account at any time in the app. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

Data sources

Map data by OpenStreetMap contributors. Weather by Open-Meteo. Place photos, where shown, come from Wikimedia Commons under their own licences and are credited.
