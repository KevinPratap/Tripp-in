Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What Trippin is

Trippin helps friends plan a trip together: suggest places, vote, and build a day by day plan.

What we collect

  • Your name, and your email if you create an account. Guests can use the app without an email.
  • The trips you create or join: places, votes, comments, preferences, plans and shared expenses. Everyone on a trip can see that trip's content and who added it.
  • Your approximate or precise location only if you allow it, to show nearby suggestions and Today mode. Step count only if you allow activity recognition. Both stay on your phone unless a feature says otherwise.

Live web page

If a trip owner turns on the live web page, a public copy of that trip is published: trip name, dates, member first names, suggested places with vote counts, and the plan. It does not include emails, expenses, comments or preferences. Anyone with the link can view it and vote with a name they type. The owner can turn it off at any time, and deleting the trip removes it.

Who processes it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Place data comes from OpenStreetMap and weather from Open-Meteo; requests to them include the coordinates of the area you are planning, not your name or email. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Your choices

  • Edit your name any time in You.
  • Delete your account in You, then Delete account and data. Trips you created are deleted for everyone on them and you leave trips you only joined.
  • Turn off location, activity or notification permissions in your phone settings.
